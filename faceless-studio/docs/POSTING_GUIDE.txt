POSTING GUIDE (one page)
========================

WHAT YOU GET
- Every morning the PC makes the day's posts and saves them in Google Drive:
  Faceless Accounts / <account> / 02 Ready Posts / <week> / <day> / <time>_<type>_<name>
- Each post folder has the images (1.jpg, 2.jpg...) or reel.mp4 + cover.jpg,
  and caption.txt with everything you need, in the order you paste it.
- THIS_WEEK_BOTH_ACCOUNTS.txt (top of Faceless Accounts) lists every post for
  both accounts, sorted by day and time. Use it as your Sunday checklist.

ONE-TIME SET-UP
1. In the Instagram app, for each account: Settings > Account type and tools >
   Switch to professional account > Creator (Vice City Corner: category
   "Video game"; Footy Smart: "Sports").
2. Link each Instagram account to a Facebook Page you manage (one Page each,
   or both to one Page). In Instagram: Edit profile > Page > Connect.
3. On your PC go to business.facebook.com and check both Instagram accounts
   appear (top left account switcher).

SUNDAY (about 30 minutes)
1. Open THIS_WEEK_BOTH_ACCOUNTS.txt and each account's WEEK_SCHEDULE.txt.
   Read any SWAP notes at the top first.
2. In Meta Business Suite pick the account (top left switcher), then Planner
   (or Content > Create post / Create reel). Menu names change now and then:
   look for "Create post", "Create reel" and "Schedule".
3. CAROUSEL: Create post > tick Instagram only > Add photo/video > select
   1.jpg, 2.jpg... in order > paste the CAPTION block, then a blank line and
   the HASHTAGS line > Schedule > set the POST AT date and time > Schedule.
4. REEL: Create reel > upload reel.mp4 > set cover.jpg as the cover (Cover >
   Upload image) > paste CAPTION + HASHTAGS > Schedule at POST AT.
5. Tick the Status box in WEEK_SCHEDULE.txt as you go.
6. Switch account (top left) and repeat for the other one.

AFTER EACH POST GOES LIVE (phone, 2 minutes)
- Post the FIRST COMMENT from caption.txt and pin it.
- Share the post to your Story using the STORY IDEA (poll/quiz sticker).
- Reply to comments in the first hour.
- Carousels: Instagram can't schedule music on photo posts from Business Suite.
  If you want music, add it in the app using the MUSIC mood in caption.txt.

BREAKING POSTS
- If the PC notification says BREAKING, open the BREAKING folder, post that
  folder from your phone straight away, then delete or move the scheduled post
  named in the SWAP note (Business Suite > Planner > the post > Edit/Delete).

BEFORE EVERY POST
- Read CHECK BEFORE POSTING in caption.txt. It flags anything that could change
  (prices, times, rumours). If in doubt, leave it out.

STATS (Sunday, 10 minutes)
- In Instagram Insights, copy each post's reach, likes, comments, saves, shares
  and follows into stats.csv in that account's 02 Ready Posts folder. The Sunday
  run reads it and plans more of what works.
