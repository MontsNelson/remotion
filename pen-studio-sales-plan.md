# 3D Pen Studio — advertising production prompt and sales plan

## Copy this prompt into your coding agent

Act as my creative director, Remotion video editor and ecommerce operator. Create sales advertising for 3D Pen Studio, a UK single-product Shopify shop selling a 3D printing pen. Deliver actual editable assets and rendered videos where tools allow, not only suggestions.

### Product and commercial facts
The owner confirms that they have the product, that it is safe and ready to deliver. Treat this as owner confirmation, not independent certification. Do not restart generic product selection or build another store. Ask only for specific missing information that affects an ad or delivery promise: exact contents and colour, approved selling price, available quantity, dispatch arrangement and delivery charge/time. Do not infer that having one pen means having stock for every order. Resolve whether orders ship from the owner or the dropshipping supplier.

Use the exact physical pen in the owner's photographs/footage. Supplier candidate previously used: Wholesale Finds UK DS-0166. Verify that footage matches the unit offered before using it. Do not invent low-temperature, age suitability, certifications, reviews, scarcity, discounts or included accessories. Do not present generated demonstrations as real product performance. Do not reuse competitor images, videos or testimonials.

### Repository workflow
Use MontsNelson/remotion and the pen-studio-sales-plan branch. Read root and applicable nested AGENTS.md instructions before editing. This repository is the Remotion source monorepo: inspect the existing example composition setup before placing video code; avoid changing core libraries to make an advert. Use bunx as required by the repository. Keep ad compositions and campaign documentation isolated. Check current Remotion licensing before commercial rendering; do not purchase anything automatically. Use FFmpeg only when it materially helps encoding/export.
Never commit customer data, addresses, credentials, private budget ledgers or licensed raw assets without permission to publish them. The repository is public. Store confidential business material privately.

### Creative direction
Premium but approachable creative gadget. Cream #FFF9F1, deep teal #16322F, coral #FF7156. Clean bold typography, readable captions, restrained transitions. The actual pen and what it makes must dominate. Target UK adult hobbyists and adult gift buyers; do not target young children or imply a verified child age rating.

Create three concepts, each with two opening hooks:
1. Transformation: "This started as a line." / "Watch this sketch leave the page."
2. Hands-on hobby: "Your next screen-free project." / "What would you make first?"
3. Honest first attempt: "My first try with a 3D pen." / "From outline to little object."
Only use first-person claims when backed by the owner's real filmed experience.

For each of six variants deliver:
- 15-second 1080 x 1920, 30 fps MP4, H.264; optional 25-second explanation.
- Shot-by-shot timing, on-screen copy, voiceover, caption and CTA.
- Editable Remotion composition with configurable footage, hook, approved price and destination.
- Licensed music or natural sound, plus a silent-caption version; no unlicensed trending tracks.
- Thumbnail and asset/rights manifest.
- Keep text within platform-safe areas, confirm current placement overlays, check legibility at phone size.
- No flashing effects. Show safe handling and label accelerated footage as time-lapse.
If footage is unavailable, make a clearly identified photo-based product ad from authorised images and provide the missing-shot list. Do not claim that finished videos exist unless rendered and inspected.

Core 15-second structure:
0–2s: finished object and real pen together, opening hook.
2–6s: close-up of tracing a simple shape.
6–10s: cooling, lifting and joining, using actual recorded steps.
10–12s: finished result held next to the pen; examples are not included.
12–15s: brand and "Explore the 3D pen — link in bio" for organic; "Shop now" only when the destination accepts orders. Show price/delivery only after approved.

### Store and selling
Audit the existing Shopify shop rather than replacing it. Prepare one clear product offer with exact contents, real options, price, delivery and returns, a purchase control, product gallery, concise FAQs and support links. Remove prelaunch copy once the actual purchase path is ready. Verify payment readiness and checkout using a non-paid test method. Do not place real paid orders without approval.
Prepare changes for review. Publishing the theme and removing password protection still require final owner approval. Do not launch paid campaigns, subscriptions or purchase assets without approval of exact costs.

### Launch deliverables
Produce a 14-day calendar, six ad variants, captions, a filming checklist, an asset register, order workflow, customer-support templates and a measurement sheet.
Track sessions, product views, add-to-cart, checkout starts, paid orders, refunds, spend and contribution profit. Assign UTM content labels to creative variants and use consent-aware analytics. Do not label attributed orders as guaranteed incremental sales.
Use the owner's current private budget and verified unit costs to compute contribution margin and break-even acquisition cost. Start with organic posts; propose paid testing only if platform minimums fit the approved budget. No revenue guarantees.
Report actual completed work, links and remaining inputs. Do not call an untested checkout or unrendered video complete.

## 14-day execution plan

Goal: obtain evidence of demand and, once checkout is ready, the first profitable orders. This is a test, not an earnings forecast.

| 2 | Film 8 short clips: pen close-up, full contents, controls, loading, tracing, cooling/lifting, joining, final object | Original asset folder and rights log |
| 3 | Assemble three ad concepts, each with two hooks | Six editable ad variants |
| 4 | Check mobile product page, cart, delivery calculation and test checkout; prepare final publish review | Test record and working destination |

If the shop cannot take payments by day 5, continue filming and editing; move sales CTAs and paid spend until checkout works. No public-posting or outreach action is implied by this document alone.

## Filming brief
Phone camera, vertical orientation, clean table, window light, steady support. Capture 3–5 seconds before and after each action. Include the pen and your hands together so the demonstration is credible. Follow product instructions and show a suitable work surface. Record the real creation time; label edited time-lapses. Film the actual contents rather than staging extra accessories.

## Starter captions
- Transformation: "A line, a little patience, and something you made yourself. What would you try first? Explore the 3D pen via the link in bio."
- Hobby: "A hands-on idea for your next creative afternoon. Start with a simple shape and see where it takes you. Product details in bio."
- First attempt (only with authentic owner footage): "My first go with this 3D pen. Keeping it simple and learning as I go. See the pen and what's included through the link in bio."

## Economics worksheet
Keep actual business figures in a private ledger.
Contribution before advertising = customer revenue (including delivery collected, excluding VAT owed where applicable) minus product cost, supplier/carrier delivery, packing, payment fees, expected return/fault costs and other variable costs.
Break-even CAC equals that contribution; choose a lower target CAC to leave room for overhead and profit.
If an approved test reaches its capped spend with no paid orders, pause and inspect creative, traffic and checkout. Zero orders in a small sample is not proof of no demand.
Do not scale on views alone. Calculate actual CAC and contribution after refunds, and preserve enough cash to fulfil outstanding orders before payouts arrive.

## Order workflow
1. Check that payment succeeded; use the Shopify order ID as the unique reference.
2. Confirm stock, address, exact option and cancellation status.
3. For own stock: pick, pack and purchase postage. For dropshipping: check a supplier order does not already exist, then order the exact option to the customer's address using authorised access.
4. Save the supplier/order reference privately. Do not put customer data in GitHub.
5. Mark fulfilled only after physical dispatch; add correct carrier/tracking and notify the customer.
6. If unavailable or delayed, contact the customer with accurate options; do not substitute silently. Follow the store's cancellation/refund process.
7. Check delivery exceptions and retain enough funds for refunds.
