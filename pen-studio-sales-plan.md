## Economics worksheet
Keep actual business figures in a private ledger.
Contribution before advertising = customer revenue (including delivery collected, excluding VAT owed where applicable) minus product cost, supplier/carrier delivery, packing, payment fees, expected return/fault costs and other variable costs.
Break-even CAC equals that contribution; choose a lower target CAC to leave room for overhead and profit.
If an approved test reaches its capped spend with no paid orders, pause and inspect creative, traffic and checkout. Zero orders in a small sample is not proof of no demand.
Do not scale on views alone. Calculate actual CAC and contribution after refunds, and preserve enough cash to fulfil outstanding orders before payouts arrive.

## Order workflow
1. Check that payment succeeded; use the Shopify order ID as the unique reference.
2. Confirm stock, address, exact option and cancellation status.
3. Supplier dispatch only: check a supplier order does not already exist, then order the exact option to the customer's address using authorised access and approved payment authority.
4. Save the supplier/order reference privately. Do not put customer data in GitHub.
5. Mark fulfilled only after physical dispatch; add correct carrier/tracking and notify the customer.
6. If unavailable or delayed, contact the customer with accurate options; do not substitute silently. Follow the store's cancellation/refund process.
7. Check delivery exceptions and retain enough funds for refunds.
